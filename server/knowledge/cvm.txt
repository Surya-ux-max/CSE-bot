CAREER PROGRESSION — JOB ROLES AFTER CSE
============================================================

Category: Career Progression
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
Graduates of the Computer Science and Engineering programme can pursue a wide range of roles in leading technology companies, startups, government organizations, and research institutions across domains like AI, cloud computing, cybersecurity, data science, and software engineering.

Description:
The Computer Science and Engineering programme prepares students for diverse career opportunities in the technology industry. Graduates develop the technical knowledge, analytical thinking, and practical skills required to solve real-world computing challenges.

Students can work in multinational companies, startups, government organizations, and research institutions. They may also pursue higher education, professional certifications, research, and entrepreneurship.

Graduates can pursue the following roles:
Software Engineer, Full Stack Developer, Web Developer, Artificial Intelligence Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, Data Analyst, System Analyst, Software Architect, Network Engineer, Database Administrator (DBA), Cybersecurity Engineer, Cloud Engineer, DevOps Engineer, Research Engineer, and Technology Consultant.

MISSION — DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING
============================================================

Category: Department Mission
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
The mission of the Computer Science and Engineering department is to create a high-quality academic environment, empower faculty and students through continuous learning, promote research and emerging technologies, and foster leadership, creativity, teamwork, and entrepreneurial thinking.

Description:
The mission of the Computer Science and Engineering department is defined through five key statements:

Mission 1: Create a high-quality academic environment supported by modern IT infrastructure and emerging technologies.

Mission 2: Empower faculty members and students through continuous learning, innovation, and industry-oriented education.

Mission 3: Promote lifelong learning, research, and the adoption of emerging technologies.

Mission 4: Foster leadership, creativity, teamwork, and entrepreneurial thinking among students.

Mission 5: Provide opportunities for students to become competent software professionals who contribute responsibly to society through technology and innovation.
