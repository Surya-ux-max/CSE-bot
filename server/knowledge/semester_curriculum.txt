SEMESTER I — FOUNDATION OF ENGINEERING AND PROGRAMMING
============================================================

Category: Semester Curriculum
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
Semester: 1

Summary:
Semester I introduces students to engineering fundamentals, C programming, mathematics, technical English, and physics, laying the foundation for all future coursework in the CSE programme.

Description:
Semester I is the first semester of the B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme under Regulation 2023. It is titled Foundation of Engineering and Programming. The semester builds mathematical, programming, and communication fundamentals.

Core Subjects taught in Semester I are:
Calculus and Linear Algebra, Problem Solving using C, Technical English, Physics for Information Science, and Application Design and Development.

Laboratories offered in Semester I are:
Problem Solving using C Laboratory and Engineering Practices Laboratory.

Professional Development activity in Semester I is:
Soft Skills.

Mandatory Course in Semester I is:
Heritage of Tamils.

Students develop skills in C programming, mathematical thinking, technical communication, and basic problem-solving during Semester I.

Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Professional Development activity in Semester III is:
Advanced Logical Thinking.

Students develop skills in algorithm analysis, database design, SQL programming, Java application development, and software engineering principles during Semester III.

Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
Semester: 5

Summary:
Semester V focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and embedded systems, and includes the summer internship, marking the students' transition into advanced and emerging technology domains.

Description:
Semester V is the fifth semester of the B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme. It is titled Intelligent Systems and Data. This is the semester where students study AI and ML, and they also complete their Summer Internship.

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML), Data Science, Computer Networks, Microprocessors and Embedded Systems, and Open Elective I.

Communication Skills and Presentation.

The Summer Internship is a mandatory component completed during Semester V or the break following Semester V.

Students develop skills in AI model development, machine learning algorithms, data analysis, Python for data science, embedded systems, and professional communication during Semester V.
