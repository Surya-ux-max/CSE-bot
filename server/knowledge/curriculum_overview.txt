Programme Objective:
The Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Engineering is designed to provide strong theoretical knowledge, practical skills, industry exposure, project-based learning, internships, research opportunities, and professional development.

The curriculum combines:

- Computer Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Cloud Computing
- Cyber Security
- Software Engineering
- Data Science
- Full Stack Development
- Engineering Sciences
- Humanities
- Laboratories
- Projects
- Professional Development
- Industry-Oriented Courses
- Internships

Students complete the programme through:

- Core Courses
- Laboratories
- Projects
- Professional Electives
- Open Electives
- Mandatory Courses
- Professional Development Courses
- Summer Internship
