CORPORATE ADVISORY BOARD
Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
============================================================

Committee Name:
Corporate Advisory Board

Department:
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Overview:
The Corporate Advisory Board of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) comprises experienced professionals from leading technology companies. The board provides industry insights, technical guidance, and strategic recommendations to ensure that the curriculum remains aligned with current industry requirements and emerging technologies.

Purpose:
The Corporate Advisory Board supports the department by connecting academia with industry. The board helps improve curriculum quality, promotes industry collaboration, recommends modern technologies, and prepares students with the practical skills required for successful careers in software engineering and information technology.

Objectives:
- Provide industry insights.
- Offer technical guidance.
- Recommend curriculum improvements.
- Support industry-academia collaboration.
- Share knowledge and best practices.
- Prepare students for industry careers.
- Promote innovation and emerging technologies.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
============================================================

The Corporate Advisory Board supports the Department of Computer Science and Engineering by:

Curriculum Development:
- Providing guidance on curriculum design.
- Recommending curriculum revisions based on industry requirements.
- Aligning academic programmes with current industry expectations.

Emerging Technologies:
- Recommending emerging technologies.
- Suggesting industry best practices.
- Supporting technology-driven curriculum enhancement.

Industry-Academia Collaboration:
- Bridging the gap between academia and industry.
- Encouraging collaboration between educational institutions and technology companies.
- Promoting knowledge sharing.

Student Development:
- Supporting internships.
- Encouraging industrial training.
- Organizing workshops.
- Delivering guest lectures.
- Providing mentoring opportunities.

Employability Enhancement:
- Improving students' employability.
- Aligning academic learning with real-world software engineering practices.
- Preparing students for careers in software and information technology.

Innovation and Research:
- Advising the department on future technology trends.
- Supporting innovation.
- Encouraging research opportunities.
- Identifying future industry requirements.

============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================

The Corporate Advisory Board strengthens the Computer Science and Engineering programme by ensuring that students receive industry-relevant education, exposure to modern technologies, and guidance from professionals working in leading global technology companies.

The board contributes to curriculum development, technical guidance, industry collaboration, mentoring, innovation, internships, industrial training, workshops, and career readiness. Their expertise helps prepare graduates to become industry-ready software professionals capable of meeting evolving technological and business challenges.
