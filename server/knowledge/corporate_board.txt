CORPORATE ADVISORY BOARD — OVERVIEW
============================================================

Category: Corporate Advisory Board
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
The Corporate Advisory Board of the CSE Department comprises experienced professionals from leading technology companies including Microsoft, Amazon, 1CloudHub, and Zoho Corporation, who provide industry guidance, curriculum recommendations, and support for student development.

Description:
The Corporate Advisory Board of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering comprises experienced technology professionals from leading companies. The board provides industry insights, technical guidance, and strategic recommendations to ensure the curriculum remains aligned with current industry requirements and emerging technologies.

The board supports the department through curriculum development, recommending emerging technologies, bridging the gap between academia and industry, supporting internships, organizing guest lectures and workshops, and helping prepare students for careers in software engineering and information technology.

Board members: Mr. Sachin Sharma (Microsoft), Mr. Akhil Anand (Amazon), Mr. UmaShankar (1CloudHub), Mr. Vishnu Prasad (Zoho Corporation).
