Category: Industry Oriented Courses
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
Industry-Oriented Courses in the CSE programme are elective subjects offered in Semesters VII and VIII that provide students with practical exposure to technologies like AngularJS, TensorFlow, LLMs, Ethical Hacking, Kotlin, PaaS, Virtualization, CRM, and Network Administration that are actively used in the technology industry.

Description:
Industry-Oriented Courses are specialized elective subjects in the B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme. They provide students with practical exposure to current industry technologies, tools, frameworks, and real-world practices. These courses are designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements, making students job-ready with technology skills that companies need.

Industry-Oriented Courses are offered in Semester VII (Industry-Oriented Course I) and Semester VIII (Industry-Oriented Course II). Students choose from the available list based on their interests and career goals.

Purpose: Expose students to industry-relevant technologies, develop practical hands-on technical skills, prepare students for professional roles in IT companies, bridge academic knowledge with industry requirements, and enhance employability.

Technologies and subjects available include:
AngularJS, TensorFlow, Large Language Models (LLMs) in Content Management, Ethical Hacking, Kotlin, Network Administration, Platform as a Service (PaaS), Virtualization, and Customer Relationship Management (CRM).

Benefits: Students gain exposure to tools used by leading IT companies, increase their employability, learn from industry-aligned content, and build expertise in in-demand technology areas.
