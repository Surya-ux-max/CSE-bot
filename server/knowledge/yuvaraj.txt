DR. S. YUVARAJ — FACULTY PROFILE
============================================================

Category: Faculty Profile
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
Dr. S. Yuvaraj is an Assistant Professor (Senior Grade) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering with over 11 years of experience, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cognitive Computing.

Description:
Dr. S. Yuvaraj is an Assistant Professor (Senior Grade) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. He has more than 11 years of experience in engineering education.

His qualifications are M.E. and he is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering at Anna University, Chennai. He completed both his Bachelor's degree and Master's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Anna University, Chennai.

Specialization: His areas of specialization are Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cognitive Computing. His research interests also include these three domains.

Research contributions: Dr. S. Yuvaraj has authored 17 research articles published in reputed conferences and peer-reviewed journals. He has also published 6 Indian patents.

Professional memberships: He is a member of IEEE and a Life Member of ISTE.

Academic responsibilities: Dr. S. Yuvaraj serves as the Coordinator of the Board of Studies (BoS) of the Computer Science and Engineering Department and as the Coordinator of the Centre of Excellence (CoE) for Full Stack Application Development. He has also mentored external participants in the Smart India Hackathon (SIH).
