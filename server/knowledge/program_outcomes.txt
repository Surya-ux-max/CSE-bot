============================================================
PROGRAMME OUTCOMES (POs)
Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
============================================================

Category:
Programme Outcomes (POs)

Department:
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Overview:
Programme Outcomes (POs) describe the knowledge, skills, professional competencies, and attitudes that students are expected to acquire by the time they graduate from the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) programme.

Purpose:
The Programme Outcomes ensure that graduates possess technical expertise, professional competence, ethical responsibility, leadership, communication skills, teamwork, and lifelong learning abilities required for successful careers in engineering and technology.

============================================================
PROGRAMME OUTCOME (PO) 1
============================================================

PO Number:
PO1

Title:
Engineering Knowledge

Description:
Graduates apply knowledge of mathematics, natural sciences, computing fundamentals, engineering principles, and computer science specialization to solve complex engineering problems and develop effective computing solutions.

Key Focus Areas:

- Mathematics
- Natural Sciences
- Computing Fundamentals
- Engineering Principles
- Computer Science
- Problem Solving
- Computing Solutions

Expected Competencies:

- Strong Technical Foundation
- Engineering Knowledge
- Analytical Skills
- Computing Expertise

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

What are Programme Outcomes (POs)?

Programme Outcomes describe the knowledge, skills, competencies, and professional attributes students are expected to achieve by the time they graduate.

------------------------------------------------------------

How many Programme Outcomes are there?

There are eleven Programme Outcomes (PO1 to PO11).

What is PO1?

PO1 focuses on Engineering Knowledge and the application of mathematics, engineering principles, computing fundamentals, and computer science knowledge to solve engineering problems.
