============================================================
PROGRAMME OUTCOMES (POs) — OVERVIEW
============================================================

Category: Programme Outcomes
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
The CSE programme has eleven Programme Outcomes (PO1 to PO11) that describe the knowledge, skills, and professional competencies graduates are expected to possess, covering engineering knowledge, problem solving, design, research, tool usage, ethics, teamwork, communication, project management, and lifelong learning.

Description:
Programme Outcomes (POs) describe the knowledge, skills, professional competencies, and attitudes that students are expected to acquire by the time they graduate from the Computer Science and Engineering programme. There are eleven Programme Outcomes, from PO1 to PO11.

The Programme Outcomes ensure that graduates possess technical expertise, professional competence, ethical responsibility, leadership, communication skills, teamwork, and lifelong learning abilities for successful careers in engineering and technology.

PO1 — ENGINEERING KNOWLEDGE
============================================================

Category: Programme Outcome
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
PO1 focuses on Engineering Knowledge, requiring graduates to apply knowledge of mathematics, natural sciences, computing fundamentals, and engineering principles to solve complex engineering problems and develop effective computing solutions.

Description:
PO1 is the first Programme Outcome of the Computer Science and Engineering programme. PO1 is titled Engineering Knowledge.

PO1 description: Graduates apply knowledge of mathematics, natural sciences, computing fundamentals, engineering principles, and computer science specialization to solve complex engineering problems and develop effective computing solutions.

Key focus areas: Mathematics, Natural Sciences, Computing Fundamentals, Engineering Principles, Computer Science, Problem Solving, and Computing Solutions.

Expected competencies: Strong Technical Foundation, Engineering Knowledge, Analytical Skills, and Computing Expertise.
