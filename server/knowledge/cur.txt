CURRICULUM

The Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Computer Science and Engineering follows the Outcome-Based Education (OBE) framework with the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) under Regulation 2023. The programme is completed over eight semesters and consists of core computer science subjects, engineering sciences, humanities, electives, laboratories, projects, internships, professional development courses, and industry-oriented learning.

Programme Summary

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)

Regulation: Regulation 2023

Curriculum Model:
• Outcome-Based Education (OBE)
• Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

Total Credits: 163

The curriculum is designed to provide strong foundations in computer science, software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, cybersecurity, data science, full-stack development, research, innovation, project-based learning, internships, and industry-ready skills.
