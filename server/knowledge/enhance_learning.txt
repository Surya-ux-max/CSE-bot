ENHANCED LEARNING

Category:
Enhanced Learning

Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Overview:
The Department of Computer Science and Engineering provides an enhanced learning environment through modern laboratories, Centres of Excellence (CoEs), value-added courses, and innovative teaching methodologies. These initiatives enable students to gain practical experience, develop industry-relevant skills, and stay updated with emerging technologies.

Objectives:
- Enhance practical learning.
- Improve industry readiness.
- Develop technical and professional skills.
- Promote innovation and research.
- Encourage hands-on software development.
- Support continuous learning.

============================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

What is Enhanced Learning?

Enhanced Learning is a learning ecosystem that combines laboratories, Centres of Excellence, value-added courses, and innovative teaching methods to improve practical skills and industry readiness.

How many laboratories are available?

The department provides specialized laboratories including Programming Languages, Data Structures, DAA, Software Engineering, Operating Systems, DBMS, Full Stack Development, AI & ML, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, and Blockchain Technologies.

Does the department have an Artificial Intelligence Laboratory?

Yes. The department has an Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Laboratory.

Does the department have a Cloud Computing Laboratory?

Yes. The department has a dedicated Cloud Computing Laboratory.

Does the department have a Full Stack Development Laboratory?

Yes. The department has a Full Stack Development Laboratory.

Does the department have a Cyber Security Laboratory?

Yes. The department has a Cryptography and Cyber Security Laboratory.

What Centres of Excellence are available?

The department has:
- Centre of Excellence in Full Stack Application Development
- Centre of Excellence in Cloud Computing

What value-added courses are offered?

The department offers courses including Competitive Programming, Cloud Security, Flutter and Firebase, Kali Linux, Selenium, Figma, Power BI, and Groovy on Grails DevOps.

What innovative teaching methods are used?

The department uses Coding Bootcamps, Google Colab, Virtual Laboratories, Quizizz, Scratch-based Programming, Discussion Forums, Think-Pair-Share, and Video-based Concept Learning.
