PROGRAMME ASSESSMENT COMMITTEE (PAC) — OVERVIEW
============================================================

Category: Programme Assessment Committee
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
Academic Year: 2024-2025

Summary:
The Programme Assessment Committee (PAC) of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering is responsible for monitoring academic quality, coordinating curriculum delivery, evaluating programme outcomes, and ensuring effective implementation of Outcome-Based Education (OBE).

Description:
The Programme Assessment Committee (PAC) is an academic governance body within the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, active for the academic year 2024-2025. The PAC is responsible for monitoring academic quality, coordinating curriculum delivery, evaluating Programme Outcomes (POs), supporting continuous academic improvement, and ensuring effective implementation of Outcome-Based Education (OBE).

The PAC consists of the Head of Department, the UG PAC Coordinator, Module Coordinators for each course, and Academic Coordinators for various departmental activities.

Description:
The Department of Computer Science and Engineering has Academic Coordinators who manage specific departmental activities within the Programme Assessment Committee (PAC) framework.

Board of Studies (BoS) Coordinator: Dr. S. Yuvaraj is the Coordinator of the Board of Studies (BoS) for the CSE Department.

Events Coordinator: Ms. V. Niranjani is the Events Coordinator for the CSE Department, responsible for organizing academic and technical events.

Examination Cell Coordinator: Mr. P. Arun Prakash is the Examination Cell Coordinator, managing examination-related activities.

Placement Coordinator: Dr. A. Mohanraj is the Placement Coordinator, responsible for placement activities and student career support.

NPTEL Coordinator: Ms. N. Gayathri is the NPTEL Coordinator, managing student enrolments in NPTEL online courses.

Industrial Visit Coordinator (IV Coordinator): Mr. B. Saravanan is the Industrial Visit Coordinator, organizing industrial visits for students.
