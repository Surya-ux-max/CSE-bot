Committee Name:
Programme Assessment Committee (PAC)

Academic Year:
2024–2025

Department:
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Overview:
The Programme Assessment Committee (PAC) of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering is responsible for monitoring academic quality, coordinating curriculum delivery, evaluating programme outcomes, supporting continuous improvement, and ensuring effective implementation of Outcome-Based Education (OBE).

Primary Responsibilities:
- Monitor academic quality.
- Coordinate curriculum delivery.
- Evaluate Programme Outcomes (POs).
- Support continuous academic improvement.
- Ensure effective implementation of Outcome-Based Education (OBE).

SUMMARY
============================================================

The Programme Assessment Committee (PAC) consists of:

- Head of the Department
- UG PAC Coordinator
- Module Coordinators
- Academic Coordinators

The committee is responsible for:

- Academic Quality
- Curriculum Delivery
- Programme Outcome Assessment
- Outcome-Based Education (OBE)
- Continuous Quality Improvement
- Examination Coordination
- Placement Activities
- Industry Interaction
- Industrial Visits
- NPTEL Coordination
- Academic Events
