Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)

The Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) define the long-term achievements expected from graduates of the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) programme after completing their degree. These objectives emphasize technical competence, continuous learning, innovation, ethical responsibility, and professional growth.

PEO1: Professional Career Development
Graduates will build successful careers in computing and information technology by applying strong foundations in engineering principles, computer science fundamentals, software development, problem-solving, and professional best practices.

PEO2: Lifelong Learning and Higher Education
Graduates will pursue higher education, research, professional certifications, and entrepreneurial ventures by demonstrating self-directed learning, adaptability, and the ability to continuously learn emerging technologies, industry trends, and evolving societal needs.

PEO3: Innovation and Ethical Computing
Graduates will conceive, design, develop, and implement innovative computing solutions that address real-world industrial and societal challenges while maintaining ethical standards, professional responsibility, sustainability, and social awareness.

The Programme Educational Objectives ensure that graduates become technically skilled professionals, lifelong learners, responsible innovators, and contributors to the advancement of technology and society.
