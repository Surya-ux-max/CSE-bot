============================================================
PROGRAMME DETAILS
Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
============================================================

Category:
Programme Details

Section:
Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)

Department:
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Overview:
The Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) define the long-term achievements expected from graduates of the Computer Science and Engineering programme after completing their degree.

The objectives emphasize:

- Technical Competence
- Professional Career Development
- Continuous Learning
- Higher Education
- Innovation
- Ethical Responsibility
- Professional Growth
- Social Responsibility
- Sustainability

Purpose:
The Programme Educational Objectives guide graduates toward successful careers, lifelong learning, innovation, ethical computing, and meaningful contributions to society.

============================================================
PROGRAMME EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVE (PEO) 1
============================================================

PEO Number:
PEO1

Title:
Professional Career Development

Objective:
Graduates will build successful careers in computing and information technology by applying strong foundations in engineering principles, computer science fundamentals, software development, problem-solving, and professional best practices.

Key Focus Areas:

- Computing
- Information Technology
- Engineering Principles
- Computer Science Fundamentals
- Software Development
- Problem Solving
- Professional Best Practices

Expected Outcomes:

- Successful Professional Career
- Strong Technical Foundation
- Industry Readiness
- Professional Competence

============================================================
PROGRAMME EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVE (PEO) 2
============================================================

PEO Number:
PEO2

Title:
Lifelong Learning and Higher Education

Objective:
Graduates will pursue higher education, research, professional certifications, and entrepreneurial ventures by demonstrating self-directed learning, adaptability, and the ability to continuously learn emerging technologies, industry trends, and evolving societal needs.

Key Focus Areas:

- Higher Education
- Research
- Professional Certifications
- Entrepreneurship
- Self-Directed Learning
- Adaptability
- Emerging Technologies
- Industry Trends
- Societal Needs

Expected Outcomes:

- Lifelong Learning
- Academic Growth
- Professional Development
- Entrepreneurial Mindset
- Continuous Skill Development

============================================================
PROGRAMME EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVE (PEO) 3
============================================================

PEO Number:
PEO3

Title:
Innovation and Ethical Computing

Objective:
Graduates will conceive, design, develop, and implement innovative computing solutions that address real-world industrial and societal challenges while maintaining ethical standards, professional responsibility, sustainability, and social awareness.

Key Focus Areas:

- Innovation
- Computing Solutions
- Software Design
- Software Development
- Industrial Challenges
- Societal Challenges
- Ethical Standards
- Professional Responsibility
- Sustainability
- Social Awareness

Expected Outcomes:

- Innovative Solutions
- Ethical Computing
- Responsible Engineering
- Sustainable Development
- Social Impact

============================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

What are Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)?

Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) define the long-term achievements expected from graduates after completing the Computer Science and Engineering programme.

------------------------------------------------------------

How many Programme Educational Objectives are there?

There are three Programme Educational Objectives:

- PEO1
- PEO2
- PEO3

------------------------------------------------------------

What is PEO1?

PEO1 focuses on Professional Career Development by applying engineering principles, computer science fundamentals, software development, problem-solving, and professional best practices.

------------------------------------------------------------

What is PEO2?

PEO2 focuses on Lifelong Learning and Higher Education through research, higher studies, certifications, entrepreneurship, and continuous learning of emerging technologies.

------------------------------------------------------------

What is PEO3?

PEO3 focuses on Innovation and Ethical Computing by developing innovative computing solutions while maintaining ethical standards, sustainability, professional responsibility, and social awareness.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme encourage higher education?

Yes. PEO2 encourages graduates to pursue higher education, research, professional certifications, and entrepreneurship.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme focus on innovation?

Yes. PEO3 emphasizes innovation, real-world problem solving, ethical computing, sustainability, and responsible technology development.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme promote lifelong learning?

Yes. Lifelong learning is one of the major objectives of PEO2.
