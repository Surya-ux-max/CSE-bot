Overview:
The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) programme provides students with a comprehensive learning experience that combines theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and industry-oriented training.

Learning Objectives:
- Build strong theoretical foundations.
- Develop practical programming skills.
- Prepare students for industry.
- Promote innovation and research.
- Encourage hands-on learning.
- Strengthen problem-solving abilities.
- Develop teamwork and professional skills.

============================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

What is the learning scope of the CSE programme?

The Computer Science and Engineering programme provides a comprehensive learning experience combining theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and industry-oriented training.

What core subjects are covered in the CSE programme?

The curriculum includes Object-Oriented Programming, Computer Architecture, Computer Networks, Database Management Systems, Operating Systems, Data Structures, Algorithms, and Software Engineering.

Does the programme include Artificial Intelligence?

Yes. Students learn Artificial Intelligence as part of the curriculum.

Does the programme include Machine Learning?

Yes. Machine Learning is one of the emerging technologies covered.

Does the programme include Cloud Computing?

Yes. Cloud Computing is included in the curriculum.

Does the programme include Cybersecurity?

Yes. Cybersecurity is one of the emerging technology domains taught.

Does the programme include Data Science?

Yes. Students learn Data Science as part of the programme.

Which programming languages are taught?

Students learn C, Python, and Java.

Is Python included in the curriculum?

Yes. Python is taught, including Python for Data Science.

Does the programme include Java?

Yes. Students learn Java and Java Full Stack Development.

Does the department provide industry laboratories?

Yes. The department provides industry-powered NextGen laboratories in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Cloud Technologies, Data Science, and Design Thinking.

What practical learning opportunities are available?

Students participate in projects, experiments, internships, hackathons, and collaborative research.

How does the programme prepare students for industry?

The programme combines theoretical learning, practical training, modern laboratories, experienced faculty, internships, research, and industry-oriented learning to develop industry-ready skills.
