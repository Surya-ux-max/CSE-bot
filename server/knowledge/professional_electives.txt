============================================================
PROFESSIONAL ELECTIVES — OVERVIEW
============================================================

Category: Professional Electives
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
Professional Electives in the CSE programme allow students to specialize in specific technology domains across seven specialization tracks: Programming, AI and Machine Learning, Data Science, Cloud Computing and DevOps, Cybersecurity, Full Stack and Web Development, and Internet of Things and Embedded Systems.

Description:
Professional Electives are specialized courses in the B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme that allow students to deepen their knowledge in a specific technology domain. Students choose from multiple specialization tracks based on their interests and career goals.

Professional Electives are offered in:
Semester VI (Professional Elective I), Semester VII (Professional Elective II and Professional Elective III), and Semester VIII (Professional Elective IV).

Students complete a total of four Professional Electives across the programme.

Available specialization tracks: Programming, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Data Science and Analytics, Cloud Computing and DevOps, Cybersecurity, Full Stack and Web Development, and Internet of Things and Embedded Systems.
