PROGRAMME SCOPE — COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING
============================================================

Category: Programme Scope
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
The Computer Science and Engineering programme prepares students to build innovative software solutions, solve real-world technological challenges, and develop industry-ready skills across core computing, AI, cloud, cybersecurity, and data science domains.

Description:
The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) programme prepares students to build innovative software solutions and solve real-world technological challenges. Computer Science is one of the fastest-growing engineering disciplines and forms the foundation of today's digital world.

The programme equips students with strong theoretical knowledge, practical skills, industry exposure, and an innovation mindset required for careers in software engineering and emerging technologies.

Programme goals: Build innovative software solutions, solve real-world technological challenges, develop strong technical foundations, prepare students for industry, encourage innovation and research, and support continuous technological learning.

Core knowledge areas: Software Development, Algorithms, Database Management Systems (DBMS), Computer Networks, and Operating Systems.

Advanced knowledge areas: Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and Emerging Technologies.

Practical learning is provided through Laboratories, Industry Projects, Internships, Hackathons, and Research Activities.

The curriculum is aligned with current industry standards and prepares graduates for future technological advancements, bridging academic knowledge with practical applications.

Future technology domains covered: Artificial Intelligence, Automation, Digital Transformation, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and Emerging Computing Technologies.
