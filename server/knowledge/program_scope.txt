============================================================
PROGRAMME SCOPE
Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
============================================================

Category:
Programme Scope

Department:
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Overview:
The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) programme prepares students to build innovative software solutions and solve real-world technological challenges. Computer Science is one of the fastest-growing engineering disciplines and forms the foundation of today's digital world.

Purpose:
The programme equips students with strong theoretical knowledge, practical skills, industry exposure, and an innovation mindset required for careers in software engineering and emerging technologies.

Programme Goals:
- Build innovative software solutions.
- Solve real-world technological challenges.
- Develop strong technical foundations.
- Prepare students for industry.
- Encourage innovation and research.
- Support continuous technological learning.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

What is the programme scope of Computer Science and Engineering?

The CSE programme prepares students to build innovative software solutions, solve real-world technological challenges, and develop industry-ready technical skills.

------------------------------------------------------------

What subjects are covered in the programme?

The programme covers Software Development, Algorithms, Database Management Systems, Computer Networks, Operating Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and Emerging Technologies.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme include Artificial Intelligence?

Yes. Artificial Intelligence is one of the advanced areas covered in the programme.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme include Machine Learning?

Yes. Machine Learning is included as an advanced technology area.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme include Cloud Computing?

Yes. Cloud Computing is one of the core emerging technology domains.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme include Cybersecurity?

Yes. Cybersecurity is covered as part of the curriculum.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the programme provide practical learning?

Yes. Students gain practical experience through laboratories, industry projects, internships, hackathons, and research activities.

------------------------------------------------------------

Does the curriculum prepare students for industry?

Yes. The curriculum is designed to meet current industry standards and prepare graduates for future technological advancements.

------------------------------------------------------------

How does the programme prepare students for future technologies?

The programme develops technical skills, innovation, problem-solving ability, adaptability, and knowledge of emerging technologies including AI, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Automation, and Digital Transformation.
