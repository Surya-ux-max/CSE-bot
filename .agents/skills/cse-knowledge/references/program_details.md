# PROGRAMME EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES (PEOs) — OVERVIEW

Category: Programme Educational Objectives
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
The Computer Science and Engineering programme has three Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) that define the long-term achievements expected from graduates: Professional Career Development (PEO1), Lifelong Learning and Higher Education (PEO2), and Innovation and Ethical Computing (PEO3).

Description:
Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) define the long-term achievements expected from graduates of the Computer Science and Engineering programme after completing their degree. There are three Programme Educational Objectives: PEO1, PEO2, and PEO3.

The objectives emphasize technical competence, professional career development, continuous learning, higher education, innovation, ethical responsibility, professional growth, social responsibility, and sustainability.

## PEO1 — PROFESSIONAL CAREER DEVELOPMENT

Category: Programme Educational Objective
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
PEO1 focuses on Professional Career Development, expecting graduates to build successful careers in computing and information technology by applying strong engineering foundations, computer science fundamentals, software development skills, and professional best practices.

Description:
PEO1 is the first Programme Educational Objective of the Computer Science and Engineering programme. PEO1 is titled Professional Career Development.

PEO1 objective: Graduates will build successful careers in computing and information technology by applying strong foundations in engineering principles, computer science fundamentals, software development, problem-solving, and professional best practices.

Key focus areas of PEO1 include: Computing, Information Technology, Engineering Principles, Computer Science Fundamentals, Software Development, Problem Solving, and Professional Best Practices.

Expected outcomes of PEO1: Successful Professional Career, Strong Technical Foundation, Industry Readiness, and Professional Competence.

## PEO2 — LIFELONG LEARNING AND HIGHER EDUCATION

Category: Programme Educational Objective
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
PEO2 focuses on Lifelong Learning and Higher Education, expecting graduates to pursue higher studies, research, professional certifications, and entrepreneurship through continuous self-directed learning and adaptability.

Description:
PEO2 is the second Programme Educational Objective of the Computer Science and Engineering programme. PEO2 is titled Lifelong Learning and Higher Education.

PEO2 objective: Graduates will pursue higher education, research, professional certifications, and entrepreneurial ventures by demonstrating self-directed learning, adaptability, and the ability to continuously learn emerging technologies, industry trends, and evolving societal needs.

Key focus areas of PEO2 include: Higher Education, Research, Professional Certifications, Entrepreneurship, Self-Directed Learning, Adaptability, Emerging Technologies, Industry Trends, and Societal Needs.

Expected outcomes of PEO2: Lifelong Learning, Academic Growth, Professional Development, Entrepreneurial Mindset, and Continuous Skill Development.

## PEO3 — INNOVATION AND ETHICAL COMPUTING

Category: Programme Educational Objective
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
PEO3 focuses on Innovation and Ethical Computing, expecting graduates to conceive, design, and implement innovative computing solutions for real-world problems while maintaining ethical standards, professional responsibility, sustainability, and social awareness.

Description:
PEO3 is the third Programme Educational Objective of the Computer Science and Engineering programme. PEO3 is titled Innovation and Ethical Computing.

PEO3 objective: Graduates will conceive, design, develop, and implement innovative computing solutions that address real-world industrial and societal challenges while maintaining ethical standards, professional responsibility, sustainability, and social awareness.

Key focus areas of PEO3 include: Innovation, Computing Solutions, Software Design, Software Development, Industrial Challenges, Societal Challenges, Ethical Standards, Professional Responsibility, Sustainability, and Social Awareness.

Expected outcomes of PEO3: Innovative Solutions, Ethical Computing, Responsible Engineering, Sustainable Development, and Social Impact.
