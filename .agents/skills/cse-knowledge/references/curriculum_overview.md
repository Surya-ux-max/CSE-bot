# CURRICULUM OVERVIEW — B.E. COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING

Category: Curriculum Overview
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Summary:
The B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme is a four-year, eight-semester degree offered under Regulation 2023 following the Outcome-Based Education (OBE) framework and the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), requiring a total of 163 credits for completion.

Description:
The Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Engineering is a comprehensive undergraduate programme designed to equip students with strong theoretical knowledge, practical skills, industry exposure, project-based learning, internships, research opportunities, and professional development.

The programme is governed by Regulation 2023 and follows the Outcome-Based Education (OBE) model, which focuses on what students are able to do by the time they graduate. The Credit system followed is the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), which gives students the flexibility to choose elective courses.

Programme details:
Programme Name: Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Computer Science and Engineering
Department: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
Regulation: Regulation 2023
Curriculum Framework: Outcome-Based Education (OBE)
Credit System: Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)
Programme Duration: 4 Years
Total Semesters: 8
Total Credits: 163

The curriculum combines core computer science subjects, artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud computing, cyber security, software engineering, data science, full stack development, engineering sciences, humanities, laboratories, projects, professional development, industry-oriented courses, and internships.

Students complete the programme through Core Courses, Laboratories, Projects, Professional Electives, Open Electives, Mandatory Courses, Professional Development Courses, and a Summer Internship.
