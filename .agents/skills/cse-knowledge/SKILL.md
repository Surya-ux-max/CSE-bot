# Sri Eshwar College of Engineering CSE Department Knowledge Base

This skill provides access to the official knowledge base of the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department.

## Instructions for the Agent
1. When a user asks a question about the CSE department, curriculum, professors, electives, committees, or regulations, look for the relevant document in the `references/` directory.
2. Use the `view_file` tool to read the contents of the appropriate document. The documents are:
   - `assessment_committee.md` - Info about the Programme Assessment Committee (PAC), HOD, coordinators, and module coordinators.
   - `corporate_board.md` - Info about the Corporate Advisory Board members and roles.
   - `curriculum_faq.md` - Frequently asked questions about the curriculum, credits, and syllabus.
   - `curriculum_overview.md` - General overview of the B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme.
   - `cvm.md` - Career progression (jobs after CSE), department vision, and mission.
   - `enhance_learning.md` - Value-added courses, online learning platforms, student publications, and industry certifications.
   - `industry_courses.md` - One-credit and three-credit courses offered by industry partners.
   - `learning_scope.md` - Skill development, project-based learning, and internships.
   - `professional_electives.md` - Details of professional elective specialization tracks and courses.
   - `professors.md` - Faculty members list, their designations, specializations, and emails.
   - `program_details.md` - Complete details about the B.E. CSE program under Regulation 2023.
   - `program_outcomes.md` - Program Educational Objectives (PEOs), Program Outcomes (POs), and Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs).
   - `program_scope.md` - Scope and domains of Computer Science and Engineering.
   - `semester_curriculum.md` - Detailed list of subjects/courses semester-wise (Semesters I to VIII) with credits.
   - `yuvaraj.md` - Info about specific coordinator/faculty member details.
3. If the user's question requires information from multiple documents, view all of them.
4. Synthesize the response clearly and concisely based *only* on the factual information found in these files. Do not make up any facts.
